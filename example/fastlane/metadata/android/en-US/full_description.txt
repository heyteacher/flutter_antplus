Flutter ANT+ demo app of package flutter_antplus

This app can be used as a reference for integrating ANT+ into your Flutter-based fitness products. 

In a real app, instead of displaying values, the data would be processed by an application for monitoring or training purposes.

flutter_antplus is a robust library that enables developers to add ANT+ wireless communication capabilities to their Flutter applications, allowing them to interact with a wide range of sports and fitness sensors directly from their mobile devices.
