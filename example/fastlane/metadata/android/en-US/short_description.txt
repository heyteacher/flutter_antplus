Flutter ANT+ demo app that displays the real-time values of sensors: heart rate, bike power, and cadence.

This app can be used as a reference for integrating ANT+ into your Flutter-based fitness products. 

Flutter ANT+ is a robust library that enables developers to add ANT+ wireless communication capabilities to their Flutter applications, allowing them to interact with a wide range of sports and fitness sensors directly from their mobile devices.
